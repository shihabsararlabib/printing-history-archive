The Printing Press

Invented by Johannes Gutenberg

1440

The dawn of mass communication. Gutenberg's mechanical movable-type printing press revolutionized the spread of knowledge, making books accessible to the masses and changing the course of history.

The Manual Typewriter

Invented by Christopher L. Sholes

1874

The Sholes & Glidden Type Writer was the first commercially successful typewriter. It introduced the QWERTY keyboard layout, a design intended to separate frequently used letter pairs and prevent the mechanical typebars from jamming.

The Electric Typewriter

Pioneered by Thomas Edison & James Fields Smathers

1872 - 1935

While Thomas Edison created the first electrically operated printing wheel in 1872, it was James Fields Smathers in 1914 who developed a practical power-roll electric typewriter. This technology eventually culminated in iconic, widely successful machines like the IBM Selectric, dominating offices worldwide.

The Laser Printer

Invented by Gary Starkweather

1969

Working at Xerox PARC, Starkweather conceptualized the laser printer by modifying an existing copier. By using a laser beam to draw an image directly onto the copier drum, he created a high-speed, high-resolution printing method that paved the way for modern digital desktop printers like the HP LaserJet shown here.

The 3D Printer

Invented by Chuck Hull

1984

Chuck Hull invented stereolithography, the first commercial 3D printing technology. His SLA-1 machine used UV lasers to cure liquid photopolymer resin layer by layer into solid plastic parts, laying the groundwork for the modern additive manufacturing revolution.

The Digital Press

Pioneered by Benny Landa

1993

Benny Landa revolutionized commercial printing by launching the Indigo E-Print 1000. By using liquid "ElectroInk", the digital press bypassed the need for traditional printing plates, enabling high-quality, high-speed, variable-data printing and short print runs.
